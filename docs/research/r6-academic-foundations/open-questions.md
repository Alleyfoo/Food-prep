# Open research-question register, R1-R6

This register consolidates the unresolved questions from R1-R6. Questions
stay open where the evidence is missing; none is closed here by argument.
Each question has five fields:
- **origin:** where the question was raised;
- **evidence now:** what is known;
- **status:** one of the values below;
- **matters because:** its practical significance for making dinner;
- **cheapest credible next step:** how to answer it.

Status values:
- *open*: nothing settles it;
- *partly answered*: some evidence, not enough;
- *answered in R6*: the literature settles the representational question;
  the culinary question may still be open;
- *closed by decision*: a project rule settles it, not evidence.

Questions are grouped by what they need:
- **KT:** kitchen testing;
- **SA:** supermarket availability checks;
- **FS:** food-safety evidence;
- **AR:** academic research and representation;
- **IE:** interface evaluation.

The user has obtained rutabaga and dried peas. That shows the user could buy
them once. It does not show regular availability at Lidl Finland.

## FS: food-safety evidence

Rule: no claim of safety from structure or tradition alone (R5, R6 brief).
R6 adds one industrial point (`evidence-industrial-process.md`, HACCP):
- critical limits must be validated, so a done-when check must never be
  shown as a safety limit;
- a food's history (how often it passed through the danger zone) matters to
  food-service safety (Y612). Two equal states with different histories are
  not interchangeable for safety.
