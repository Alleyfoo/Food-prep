Date read: 2026-11-25 for every Q source below.
Method: WebSearch to find pages, WebFetch to read them. WebFetch returns a
model-written summary of the page against a prompt, not the raw text, so
every Q entry is "WebFetch summary" unless stated. Numbers and short quotes
come from those summaries. Quotes are at most 15 words and only for
endpoint or failure wording.
Search languages: Finnish, Swedish, German, Polish, Russian, Turkish,
French, Italian, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, English.

### transfer onion_vessel (onion vessel, Nice/Liguria form)
1. Peel onion. Halve across (O5) or keep whole and halve after boiling (Q328, O4 hollow whole).

Overdone: layers tear/fall apart (O12, O15), slippery to handle (O10). Skipped: possible (O1, O11) but then raw-hollow onions need long braises (O6, O7).
