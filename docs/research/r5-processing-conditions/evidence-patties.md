# Evidence: grated-vegetable patties (salting courgette, peeling broccoli stem, cooking the patty)

Date read: 2026-11-25. Pairs 21-23 (`salt_grated_courgette`, `peel_broccoli_stem`,
`cook_vegetable_patty`). Evidence ids Q400-Q431 (Q430-Q431 read after the
coordinator's scope addendum; see "Sequence notes" at the end).

Method: WebSearch to find, WebFetch to read (each page read as a WebFetch summary
against a prompt asking for conditions, endpoints, failures and keeping; endpoint
wording asked for verbatim). Earlier evidence cited, not re-read: R2
`r2-construction-patterns/recipes.md` (M3a-b mücver, M4a-b kolokythokeftedes,
E18a-d morotsbiffar/porkkanapihvit, E19a-b Zucchinipuffer/placki, A9a-b hobak-jeon)
and R4 `r4-transfers/evidence-gratin-stems.md` part B (B1-B24, broccoli stem
patties). One R2 page (lecker.de Zucchinipuffer, E19a) was re-read as Q411 for
details R2 lacks (keeping, cold pan, ahead-of-time batter).

Search languages: Finnish, Swedish, Turkish, Greek, German, Japanese, English.

Traps:
- Search-engine summaries repeatedly stated conditions (e.g. "mixture releases water
  as it stands, fry at once" for a Turkish page; "Liebherr: veggie patties keep 3 days")
  that were not confirmed when a page was read. Those are listed as snippet-only and
  not used.
- Finnish big-site pages for courgette patties (K-Ruoka, Valio, Kotikokki) did not
  surface in search; Finnish courgette evidence is one Yle page (Q408) plus the
  Finland-Swedish Martha page (Q406). Finnish carrot-patty pages are plentiful.
- K-Ruoka porkkanapihvit page came back truncated (no recipe text); its "200 °C 15 min
  oven alternative" is snippet-only and not used.
- Q402 (Yemek.com) contradicts itself on fridge keeping (1-2 days vs up to 3 days);
  both kept.
- Q403 salt quantity differs between ingredient list and method on the same page.
- Q416 (Kotikokki oven carrot patties) appears to lack an ingredient (carrot simmered
  in milk with nothing to thicken it is listed); used only for oven temperature/time.

## Sources

### Q400 — https://yemek.com/mucverin-puf-noktalari/
- Turkish, Turkey; large recipe portal, editorial tips article (Cemre Özgüzel); 2019-04-25; WebFetch summary.
- [salt_grated_courgette] Do not grate the seedy, watery core of the courgette. No salting step. Squeeze: spread grated courgette on a clean cloth, gather and wring over a bowl, or press against a strainer, or squeeze handfuls between both palms. Endpoint: repeat until no water is left, "Bu işlemi kabakta su kalmayana kadar tekrar edin." Active: hands-on, continuous during squeezing.
- [salt_grated_courgette] Mixture endpoint: paste-like, "macun gibi bir kıvamda".
- [cook_vegetable_patty] Shallow-fry spoonfuls in hot oil (olive oil recommended as absorbing least); turn when one side is lightly browned; drain on paper.
- Failures and causes: falls apart from too little flour or from a melting/fatty cheese (kaşar); oil absorption from water not squeezed out (squeezing well reduces both oil uptake and crumbling); burning from fatty/melting cheese; floury taste from too much flour. Raw inside / not crisp listed as problems, cause not given.
- Keeping: not stated.

### Q401 — https://www.posta.com.tr/galeri/mucveri-boyle-yapinca-yag-cekmiyor-firindan-cikmis-gibi-hafif-oluyor-2995837
- Turkish, Turkey; newspaper food gallery, no author; 2026-05-23; WebFetch summary.
- [salt_grated_courgette] Salt sprinkled on grated courgette (amount not stated), wait at least 15 min; then squeeze as hard as possible by hand or in a clean cloth until nearly dry. Retained water named as a cause of oil uptake.
- [salt_grated_courgette] Batter endpoint: thick, not runny, "hafif koyu bir kek kıvamında olmalıdır" (slightly thick cake-batter consistency).
- [cook_vegetable_patty] Oil well heated before the batter goes in; then medium heat. Too high: outside burns, inside raw. Too low: fritters float in the oil. Cold/underheated oil: absorbs oil. Loose batter (wrong egg:flour balance) disperses when it meets the oil. Drain on a wide wire strainer.
- Keeping: not stated.

### Q402 — https://yemek.com/tarif/mucver/
- Turkish, Turkey; large recipe portal, staff chef (Yasemin Gürsürer); 2026-09-25; WebFetch summary.
- [salt_grated_courgette] 3 courgettes coarse-grated; no pre-salting (salt 1.5 tsp goes into the mixture); squeeze until the juice is fully out, FAQ: "suyunun tamamen sıkılması lazım". Grated carrot also squeezed (intro).
- [salt_grated_courgette] Mixture endpoint: mix until paste-like, "macun kıvamını alana kadar"; thick, holds its shape on the spoon, does not spread.
- [cook_vegetable_patty] ½ cup oil, very hot ("kızgın yağ"), oil pre-heated to reduce absorption; 1 heaped tbsp per fritter; fry "altın sarısı rengini alana kadar" (until golden yellow); drain on paper. Time per side not stated.
- Failures and causes: raw soft centre and not crisp because courgette water thins the batter; oil absorption from too-wet batter or oil not hot enough; falls apart → squeeze more thoroughly; too wet → add flour.
- Keeps [cook_vegetable_patty]: cool to room temperature, then fridge in an airtight container; method says 1-2 days, FAQ says up to 3 days (internal conflict). Reheating not stated.

### Q403 — https://yemek.com/tarif/firinda-kabak-mucveri/
- Turkish, Turkey; large recipe portal, editor (Ege Şahin); 2023-06-13, updated 2023-06-15; WebFetch summary including reader comments and editor replies.
- [salt_grated_courgette] 3 medium courgettes peeled, grated, squeezed well in the palm; no salting step. Tip: excess water leaves the centre undercooked.
- [cook_vegetable_patty] Oven version as one slab: batter spread on paper-lined tray, 180 °C fan, 40 min; no turning. Fan setting called essential: non-fan oven or wet courgette → undercooked centre. No doneness test given.
- Comments: one reader salted the mixture a day ahead and refrigerated it → it became watery (editor confirmed); air-fryer attempt → centre raw and pieces broke when flipped (editor: adjust temperature/time or two batches); shallow dish → centre did not cook; more flour and longer baking fixed a raw centre; two readers found it too salty (salt amount differs between list and method).
- Keeps: raw salted mixture overnight in fridge goes watery (comment, editor agrees). Cooked: not stated.

### Q431 — https://arlafoods.co.uk/recipes/carrot-fritters
- English, UK; dairy brand; 2025-03-05; WebFetch summary.
- [salt_grated_courgette by role / cook_vegetable_patty] Carrot coarsely grated, **salted with a pinch, left a few minutes to draw out moisture, drained and squeezed in paper towel** (a carrot dewatering step, against "carrot never squeezed").
- Mixture endpoint: "thick and slightly sticky, firm enough to shape into patties"; if loose, add egg or flour.
- Pan: butter + rapeseed oil, moderate 180 °C (thermometer or test drop of batter); small batches with space between them to keep the temperature steady; about 2 min per side.
- Done: golden-brown, crunchy shell, crisp edges, "crispy on the outside, tender on the inside".
- Failures and causes: soggy — moisture not removed; falls apart — mix too wet or loose; greasy — oil too cool; burnt outside, raw inside — oil too hot.
- Keeps: cool, fridge (days not stated); reheat in a preheated oven 180 °C about 5-10 min; freeze cooled in an airtight container up to 3 months, thaw in the fridge before reheating, or reheat from frozen with a few extra minutes.
