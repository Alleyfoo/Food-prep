## Recipe entries (napa / Chinese cabbage)

### N1 - Delish Kitchen: コンソメロール白菜 (consommé hakusai rolls) + reviews
- URLs: https://delishkitchen.tv/recipes/157441982522720659 and https://delishkitchen.tv/recipes/157441982522720659/reviews
- Language: Japanese. Site type: large commercial recipe-video site; reviews are user-submitted. No date shown.
- Read: WebFetch summary (both pages).
- Softening: microwave 600 W, 3-4 min, loosely wrapped; then wipe off released water ("水気が出たら拭いておく。").
- Rib: shaving not mentioned.
- Filling: 200 g pork mince, onion, breadcrumbs, milk (no rice). 4 rolls / 2 servings.
- Cooking: simmered seam-side down in 400 cc water + consommé + bacon, lid on, low heat 30 min ("煮立ったらふたをして弱火で30分煮る。").
- Quality: 4.5 stars from 123 ratings. Of 16 visible reviews: 12 positive, 4 mixed (2 too salty, 1 leaves tough + bland, 1 broth strong), 0 negative. No mention of tearing, unrolling or wateriness. One reviewer found it easier to roll than cabbage because leaves are smaller; one prefers it to the cabbage version ("キャベツで作るよりも好きです。"). One found the fibres took effort to bite through.

### N2 - Delish Kitchen: ロール白菜照り焼き (teriyaki hakusai rolls) - reviews page
- URL: https://delishkitchen.tv/recipes/172099903516836243/reviews
- Language: Japanese. Site type: commercial recipe site, user reviews. Recipe page itself NOT opened (only the reviews page).
- Read: WebFetch summary.
- Cooking (from reviews): pan-fried then glazed - not simmered or baked.
- Quality: 4.2 from 61 ratings. Problems reported: rib/core stays stiff unless thoroughly microwaved; wrapping slow; one roll set fell apart after frying (another reviewer fixed with a toothpick); SEVERAL reviewers found leaves hard to bite through, esp. outer leaves ("白菜が硬くて噛みきれず食べづらかった。"); fixes: inner leaves, cut rolls in half. No reviewer called it watery. One: looks like cabbage rolls but with an odd gooey texture.

## Synthesis

**Documented?** Yes, widely - but not in Finland or Sweden. Napa/Chinese-cabbage rolls with a meat filling are standard in Japan (ロール白菜, several Delish Kitchen recipes with 11-123 ratings: N1-N3; magazine recipe N4), Korea (N5), Germany/Austria (Chinakohlrouladen: N6-N8), Russia/Ukraine (голубцы из пекинской капусты: N10, N11), Norway (N13) and English-language sources (N9, N12). No Finnish "kiinankaalikääryleet" and no Swedish kåldolmar on kinakål/salladskål was found. N10 and N6 are the closest analogues to kaalikääryleet (mince + rice, oven).

**Softening:** much lighter than white cabbage (N0 boils the whole head ~10 min). Napa: microwave 600 W 3-5 min (N1, N3, N4, N9, N10 alt.), blanch 1-3 min (N5, N6, N11, N12), or just scald/dip (N10, N13). Wipe off released water (N1).

**Rib:** the thick white rib is the recurring weak point. Most sources shave, flatten, cut out or trim it (N3, N4, N6, N7, N8, N9, N10, N11, N12); N3/N4 mince the shavings into the filling. Where it is not handled, reviewers complain.

**Quality evidence:** Mostly positive where reviews exist (N1 4.5/123; reviewers say tastier than cabbage and easier to roll because the leaves are smaller). Main documented failure is **tough, stringy rib/stalk fibres that are hard to bite** (N2 several reviewers, N1 one, N3 one, N4 the writer) - fixed by shaving the rib, cooking longer, using inner leaves, or cutting the rolls. Second: **rolls coming apart when pan-fried** (N2), fixed with toothpicks; sources pack rolls tightly seam-side down (N1, N10). **Tearing** is rarely reported; N9 keeps spare leaves, N10 says microwaving gives tear-free leaves. **Wateriness** was not reported by any reviewer, though N1 tells you to wipe off released water. **Mushiness** when overcooked is warned in N12. Leaves are smaller than white-cabbage outer leaves; N9 overlaps two per roll.

**Effort vs white cabbage:** less pre-cooking (no whole-head boil); more leaf prep per roll (rib shaving, possibly two leaves). No source gives a measured comparison. **No direct comparison with kaalikääryleet/kåldolmar was found**; N10 says the recipe is otherwise identical to classic golubtsy, and N4 and an N1 reviewer favour it over ロールキャベツ (Japanese Western-style simmered rolls, not the Finnish dish).

**Baked vs simmered:** Baked versions are documented (N6, N7, N8, N9, N10, N11, N12 option), but **every one bakes the rolls in liquid or sauce** (tomato, sour cream/tomato, stock, cream/cheese) and often covered (N9 foil 1 h, N12 foil). The best-reviewed evidence (Japanese) is simmered. **No source bakes napa rolls the Finnish way (syrup, a splash of water, uncovered ~170 °C 40 min), so there is no quality evidence for that.** Baked-recipe ratings are few (N6 4.0/6; N7 2.5/4, rice-only filling, so the score can't be put down to the leaf). Inference, not evidence: the thin leaf edges may dry or scorch under syrup without liquid, so an R4 test would be closest to the documented practice if it keeps the rolls packed tightly and adds more liquid or covers them for part of the bake.

**Lidl Finland status:** **Unconfirmed for both.** No lidl.fi product page (/p/) for fresh kiinankaali or suippokaali was found. Kiinankaali appears only indirectly on lidl.fi (storage guide L24; Lidl recipe needing 200 g, L25; kimchi recipe snippet L26). Suippokaali has one trace: an old offer snippet (domestic, 1.29 €/kg, -35 %, max 3 kg/household, 29.9.-2.10., **no year**, page now 404: L19). Current offer pages (23.11.-2.12.) did not render product lists through the fetch tool, so absence there is not evidence. A store visit or the Lidl Plus app is needed to confirm.
