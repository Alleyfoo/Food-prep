SMALLEST TEST
RATIO
1 small piece of roasted broccoli : 1/4 teaspoon brown butter
SUCCESS
Nutty aroma reinforces browning while the roasted broccoli remains recognizable and not greasy.
LIKELY FAILURE
Fat coats the palate and suppresses the vegetable's own character.
CORRECTION
Use half as much butter; add one drop lemon; finish with salt rather than more fat.
SAFETY
Use freshly prepared brown butter without burned milk solids and fully cooked vegetables.

works Brown butter works on roasted broccoli — the hypothesis holds. Garlic confit on the same state was clearly better, so brown butter is a real option here but not the best one. Clarified the same day: the thing it lost to is garlic confit — cloves slow-roasted about two hours in ghee — not garlic butter. Ghee has had its milk proteins removed, so unlike butter it cannot brown; the two fats differ in what happened to the proteins, not merely in seasoning. The verdict is unchanged, only the identity of what it was compared against.
